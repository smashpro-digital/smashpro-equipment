SP-MZIGO-26

Remote Controlled Electric Material Carrier

SMASHPRO FLEET

Purpose Built For Productivity

The SP-MZIGO-26 is a remote controlled electric material carrier developed for the SmashPro Fleet. Designed to transport heavy materials across residential and commercial job sites safely, efficiently, and with minimal ground disturbance. Working alongside the SP-ARDHI-26 Mini Skid Loader, the SP-MZIGO-26 creates a highly productive material handling system for landscaping, construction, and property improvement projects.

Why MZIGO?

Name Origin

Mzigo is the Swahili word for Load, Cargo, or Freight. The name reflects the machine's mission: Moving materials quickly, efficiently, and safely.
