## Purpose

SP-MTC-001 turns lessons from the SP-MZIGO-26E factory build into a controlled platform for future electric material carriers. The product definition is separate from Fleet Asset SP-MZIGO-26E and Passport ID SPP-2026-0002.

The current unit is evidence for a production proof-of-concept. It has not completed arrival inspection, commissioning or field validation, and the catalog product is not offered for sale.

## Engineering evidence

The SP-MZIGO-26E Passport links dated factory evidence for the battery boxes, drive hardware, controllers, hydraulic cylinder and power unit, remote controller, electrical wiring, axles, frame, tie-down anchors and maintenance access.

Visual evidence confirms configuration and component placement. It does not establish unrecorded ratings, recovery capacity, regulatory certification or field readiness.

## QC and release gates

The supplied September 15 production status records quality inspection passed and SmashPro build approval for the Founders Edition unit. Product release still requires:

1. Manufacturer specification and operating documentation.
2. Export, delivery and arrival records for the physical unit.
3. Commissioning inspection and safe operating guidance.
4. Field validation under representative loads and terrain.
5. Controlled BOM, drawings, QC procedure and service schedule.
6. Review of safety, labeling, warranty and regulatory responsibilities.

## OEM collaboration record

Shandong Kylin Heavy Industry Machinery Co., Ltd. supplied the OEM platform and collaborated on the documented paint, branding, wheel, battery-box and tie-down changes. Future production should translate one-off requests into revision-controlled option packages while preserving OEM/manufacturer-of-record identity.
