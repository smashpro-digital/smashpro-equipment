## 5. Load planning and verification gates

- ARDHI / YF380 published base operating weight: approximately **1,940 lb**. The actual transport weight, including or replacing factory bucket/attachments, must be verified.
- Separate Loflin 42-inch Mini Root Grapple manufacturer listed weight: approximately **370 lb**; this is **not** the approximately 390-lb freight shipment weight.
- Initial planning geometry: **four independent equipment tie-down connections** for the machine and **two restraint paths** for the detached grapple. This is a concept only, not a certified cargo-securement layout. Towing/recovery points are **not automatically approved tie-down points**.
- Verify approved machine and attachment anchor points, rated trailer D-rings, wire-J hook fit and retention, load-path angle, abrasion protection, actual loaded weight, tie-down location, brake and ramp suitability, and cargo-securement laws before use. Evaluate whether simple two-point grapple restraint adequately prevents all directions of movement; add restraints as needed.
- The borrowed Moye 6 × 12-ft tandem-axle dump trailer is planned for **fence debris**. **It has not been verified as capable of transporting ARDHI or the grapple.**
- Secure loose rails, posts, sharp objects and any wire mesh using appropriate containment; straps alone do not secure loose small debris.
- Grapple remains **Delivered · Fitment Review Required**. Unresolved locking-pin engagement prevents commissioning; the ARDHI throttle-control hold and independent equipment readiness also remain separately gated.
- Do not advertise these purchased straps or equipment as tested, commissioned, safety-certified or operationally approved until evidence exists.
