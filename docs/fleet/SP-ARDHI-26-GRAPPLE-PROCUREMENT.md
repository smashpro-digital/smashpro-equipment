# SP-ARDHI-26 Grapple Procurement Record

> Status: Active comparison / no purchase commitment
> Updated: 2026-09-05

## Objective

Select the first hydraulic grapple for SP-ARDHI-26 around SmashPro's actual work: brush and vegetation cleanup, uprooted shrubs and roots, limbs and small logs, irregular debris, storm/property cleanup, and general commercial property-service cleanup.

## Machine baseline

- Fleet asset: SP-ARDHI-26
- Factory model: YF380
- Manufacturer: Shandong Infront Machinery Group Co., Ltd.
- Chassis width: 810 mm / 31.89 in
- Factory bucket width: 1,090 mm / 42.91 in
- Operating weight: 880 kg / 1,940 lb
- Rated operating load: 280 kg / 617 lb
- Maximum lifting force: 458 kg / 1,009 lb
- Hydraulic configuration: three-pump / three-valve production configuration
- Documented hydraulic pressure: 17 MPa / approximately 2,466 psi
- Auxiliary hydraulic quick connects: documented
- Exact auxiliary flow, coupler specification, reversing-circuit confirmation, and quick-attach geometry remain fitment gates until confirmed.

### Current direct-factory discussion

Contact: Greg Loflin, Loflin Fabrication

Confirmed by Greg:

- Mini Root Grapple is the recommended Loflin attachment for SmashPro's stated use cases
- Width is operator/application preference; attachment weight changes with width
- Grapple requires two reversing hydraulic lines
- Loflin noted grapple hydraulics can be used with systems up to approximately 3,500 psi
- Custom colors/designs require a 20-piece minimum
- Loflin's standard green is John Deere Green, not RAL 6018
- SmashPro finish direction is therefore standard black unless a future finish option is verified as a close RAL 6018 match
- SmashPro intends to use removable post-production branding rather than a custom-production design
- Loflin has a retail location in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina

## Candidate B — Shandong Infront / Factory-Supplied Grapple

Infront is the manufacturer of SP-ARDHI-26 and can potentially supply a grapple built around the YF380 interface with SmashPro branding/finish options.

### Strategic advantages

- Highest likelihood of direct YF380 mount compatibility if supplied for the exact machine configuration
- Existing supplier relationship and build history
- Existing SmashPro branding artwork and RAL 6018 factory color context
- Potential lower acquisition price than a U.S.-built attachment
- Potential ability to stage payments through Alibaba based on the prior SP-ARDHI-26 procurement relationship

## Preliminary decision framework

Score both candidates on:

1. Fitment certainty
2. Finished attachment weight vs ARDHI's 617 lb rated operating load
3. Brush/root/limb performance
4. Jaw geometry and visibility
5. Durability / steel construction
6. Hydraulic compatibility
7. Coupler readiness
8. Branding / finish quality
9. Delivered cost
10. Payment flexibility
11. Lead time
12. Parts / warranty support
13. U.S. serviceability
14. Content / partnership value

## Current position

The Loflin Mini Root Grapple remains the preferred U.S.-built candidate, but no order should be placed until a current Infront factory grapple quote and technical sheet are obtained and scored against Loflin on delivered cost, attachment weight, construction, fitment, support and payment flexibility.
