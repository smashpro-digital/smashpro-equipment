### Before any proposed ARDHI loading trial

1. Record SKU and physical unit label/UPC, photograph the ramp rating plates, inspect welds/rungs and both supplied retaining straps.
2. Obtain the specific manufacturer manual, verify approval for **rubber tracks** and machine configuration, and determine whether 1,500 lb per ramp is adequate under **peak dynamic and uneven contact loading**.
3. Measure actual machine weight with planned bucket/grapple, track widths/spacing, and trailer deck height/rear opening; compare against the manufacturer-approved loading configuration and calculated load paths.
4. Verify the proposed trailer has its own load rating, rear edge strength, suitable ramp attachment/retention provision, brakes, hitch and equipment securement points, independent of these ramps.
5. If ratings or compatibility cannot be documented, obtain a suitable purpose-built tracked-equipment ramp and trailer instead. Do not field-test beyond the stated rating.
