# Mission

SP-GARI-26E is reserved for a usable compact shop/support vehicle that also serves as a promotional vehicle, partner-integration build, and multi-episode content platform. It is expected to begin with an inexpensive older electric golf-cart or utility-cart donor and become a modern connected SmashPro Garage EV.

Primary roles are shop support, compact utility transport, content production, partner integrations, and promotional use. A modular rear passenger/utility configuration is preferred over a permanently recreational layout.
