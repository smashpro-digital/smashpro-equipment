# Mission

SP-INAMA-26 is SmashPro Fleet's planned specialty low-profile transport platform for ramp-free loading of compact equipment, landscape tools, attachments, and field-support assets.

Its hydraulic tilt/lowering deck is intended to reduce loading angle and setup friction while preserving an open working deck. INAMA is designed to complement, not replace, the larger U.S.-built SP-BEBA-HD-26 operational trailer.

SP-INAMA-26 remains a slower-priority acquisition behind revenue-producing equipment and the first operational heavy trailer.
