# SP-ARDHI-26 — Night Ops V1/V2 and LASFIT lighting plan

Updated: 2026-10-29. Status: planning / field prototype, not installed V2 or purchased LASFIT equipment.

## V1 baseline
Magnetic, battery-operated removable lighting being tested on delivered ARDHI. Record positions, runtime, beam coverage, vibration retention, and night footage before permanent installation.

## V2 target
Hardwired, weather-resistant auxiliary lighting, isolated secondary battery and solar charging with appropriate charge control, fused circuits, disconnect, strain relief, and protected wiring. Verify machine charging capacity, battery chemistry, load, panel placement, and safe mounting before procurement. Retain original factory headlights and momentary horn functionality.

## Switch constraints
Preserve the existing factory two-switch dashboard opening without cutting the painted panel. Investigate a 2x2 miniature-switch adapter or separate auxiliary controller. Measure cutout and rear clearance; no switch fitment confirmed. Horn must remain momentary and independently reliable.

## LASFIT partner strategy
LASFIT already supported SmashPro Garage Project Rebirth with a major product seed. ARDHI lighting is a **planned customer purchase**, not another confirmed seed. Rebirth headlight installation is delayed. Purchase a suitable universal LASFIT light first, make a documented ARDHI install/night demonstration video, then share performance and ask whether LASFIT wants to support further lighting. Check existing partner discount eligibility; do not claim an order or shipment yet.

### Product candidates, pending current listing and fitment checks
- 12-inch white V-Series combo LED light bar for front grille (initial purchase preference).
- Two compact wide-flood rear pods, preferably 3-inch class, for later phase.
- Optional white/amber rock lights; purple accent lights remain a separate aesthetic requirement.
- Matching harness, fuses, sealed connectors and mounts, selected only after electrical validation.

### Rear pod requirement
Mount one adjustable flood pod at each rear corner. Each must manually rotate and lock from rear-facing backup illumination to side-facing work illumination (at least 90 degrees horizontal swivel). Standard tilt-only brackets are insufficient. Verify clearance through lift-arm travel, operator egress, vibration, heat, and cable movement; provide independent left/right switching. Do not assume motorized swivel or automatic reverse integration.

## Partner sourcing
Power/solar, switching, and mounting sponsorships remain separate opportunities. Existing unanswered targets should not receive duplicate cold pitches; seek alternative brands. Record partner response, terms, seed vs purchase status, evidence, and attribution accurately.

## Media and release gates
1. Capture V1 magnetic night lighting baseline.
2. Measure grille mounting, voltage and draw; verify LASFIT SKU, bracket, and partner discount.
3. Purchase only after fitment and electrical review.
4. Film unboxing, installation, beam comparisons, and operating-load test.
5. Publish video and Equipment Passport case study; label LASFIT product customer-purchased.
6. Invite LASFIT to evaluate a future rotating rear-pod expansion, without representing it as approved.

No production electrical modifications or public claims of a confirmed V2 seed are authorized by this plan.
