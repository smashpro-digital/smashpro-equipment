<p><strong>At a glance:</strong> October 31, 2026 — five days after delivery — SP-ARDHI-26 worked dirt for the first time: playground-area preparation, grading, material movement and pallet-fork practice. Reported time across his early sessions is roughly five hours, including a 3+ hour stretch. That is operator-reported time, not a verified meter reading, and field work is not commissioning approval.</p>

<p>The session mixed the jobs this machine was bought for. Bucket passes moved and spread dirt; back dragging — pulling the bucket backwards with light down pressure — smoothed the graded surface between passes. We treat that technique note as a field observation, not manufacturer guidance: the YF380 operating documentation applicable to this exact configuration is still being confirmed.</p>

<h2>What Dirt Day surfaced</h2>
<figure>
  <img src="https://smashpro.app/equipment/images/sp-ardhi-26-field-qa-control-panel-2026-10-31-web.jpg" alt="Control panel with throttle lever and toothed quadrant, labels for lamp, horn, ignition lock and throttle" width="1440" height="1920" loading="lazy" />
  <figcaption>The throttle quadrant photographed during post-session checks. After Dirt Day the lever physically stops at roughly half travel.</figcaption>
</figure>
<p>Honest field evaluation means recording what went wrong. Two findings followed the session:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Throttle travel restriction.</strong> The lever cannot move past about half its range. The cause is unverified — no forcing, no governor adjustments. A replacement cable kit was ordered November 2 and is not yet installed or confirmed compatible.</li>
  <li><strong>Quick-change pin screw.</strong> One attachment locking pin lost its retaining screw, disabling the spring return. The owner has since reported the repair complete, with an additional screw added to both pins; independent retention verification is still pending before loaded attachment work.</li>
</ul>
<p>Both items, their evidence and their current status live on the <a href="https://smashpro.app/equipment/sp-ardhi-26.html">Equipment Passport's commissioning checklist</a>, and the deeper maintenance story is in ARDHI's Early Ownership Log (companion story).</p>
